# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

During this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. I was impressed by the large number of services available for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other cloud needs. AWS also provides services such as Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, and Amazon RDS, which can support different types of applications and organizations.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, object storage, databases, identity and access management, and Kubernetes services. They also offer scalable and flexible cloud resources that businesses can use without having to maintain all of their own physical infrastructure. Although their services have different names and features, they provide similar solutions for common cloud computing requirements.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because every organization has different needs. For example, a company that uses many Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while a business focused on artificial intelligence may consider GCP. Startups may prioritize cost and scalability, while large enterprises may focus more on security, reliability, performance, and global availability.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned to analyze the requirements of a business and match those requirements with the appropriate cloud services. I also gained a better understanding of how AWS, Azure, and GCP can be compared based on their capabilities.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because I now have organized documentation of my activities, cloud service comparisons, client recommendations, decision matrices, and Linux investigation. I also learned how to use GitHub to store and present my work professionally. Overall, this mission helped me connect cloud computing concepts with practical business scenarios.
